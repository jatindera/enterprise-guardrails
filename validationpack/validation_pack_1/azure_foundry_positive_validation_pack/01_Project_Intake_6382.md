### Problem Statement

Employees cannot quickly locate answers to routine internal IT support and onboarding questions scattered across approved help articles and procedural documents. The team wants a simple browser-based application with searchable articles, a small request form and an optional AI-assisted answer feature that summarizes **approved internal articles only**, shows citations and offers an escalation link when evidence is insufficient. Estimated initial usage is 60 internal users, with 10 concurrent during pilot; the exact feature boundary, data sensitivity, content ownership, identity, retention, AI behavior and service-level targets require confirmation. **No technology stack has been selected at intake.**

### Value Description

A centralized, accessible web application should reduce time spent searching for answers, preserve a human support path, and provide traceable responses. The AI-assisted answer feature is proposed to help users find relevant approved material, not to execute changes, make personnel decisions or replace support staff. Value and model quality will be measured during a pilot.
