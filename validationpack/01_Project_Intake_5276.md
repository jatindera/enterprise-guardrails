### Problem Statement

Procurement teams track supplier insurance and quality-certificate expiry dates in separate spreadsheets and shared mailboxes. Ownership is unclear when employees change roles, reminder messages are inconsistent, and users cannot reliably distinguish an expired document from a superseded one. The initial estimate is 1,200 supplier records, 200 certificate updates per month and 40 internal users across three purchasing offices. These volumes are planning estimates rather than measured production data. The team needs a controlled document register, assigned owners, expiry reminders, status history and a searchable view. Whether suppliers themselves need access, whether mobile use must work offline, retention, integration boundaries and support requirements must be clarified. A technology platform has not been selected.

### Value Description

A shared renewal tracker would reduce duplicate follow-up, make document ownership visible and help staff identify certificates requiring attention. The value comes from reliable workflow and document tracking; no requirement for generative AI, automated interpretation of certificate content or a large-scale analytics platform has been established. The solution should support a proportionate implementation and make licensing, administration and support effort visible before a platform is chosen. The tracker will not determine whether a supplier is approved or legally compliant.

| Field | Value |

| Supported Company Goals | Improve purchasing workflow visibility and reduce manual follow-up |

#### Desired Business Outcome

Provide one searchable register linking each certificate to a supplier identifier, document owner, expiry date and review status. The proposed business target is to reduce average manual follow-up effort from an estimated 20 minutes to 5 minutes per renewal and notify owners before certificates expire. The baseline and target will need validation during the pilot. Human procurement reviewers remain accountable for checking document content and deciding supplier actions. Final scope, technology, operating costs and acceptance criteria will be confirmed through stakeholder review.
